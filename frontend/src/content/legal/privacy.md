This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have under the **EU GDPR**, the **UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021)**, and equivalent frameworks.

## 1. Data We Collect
- **Account data:** name, email address, password hash, professional title, organisation, and profile details you provide.
- **Learning data:** course enrolments, lesson progress, quiz attempts, certificates earned, and streak/goal activity.
- **AI interaction data:** messages you send to the AI Tutor (Aletheia) and AI Career Mentor (Solon), retained for quality review.
- **Payment data:** processed by Stripe. Card details never touch ITHR infrastructure; we retain only the transaction reference and plan.
- **Technical data:** IP address (hashed for abuse-prevention), device/browser type, and application logs.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To deliver courses, track progress, and issue verifiable certificates.
- To operate AI tutoring and mentoring features.
- To process payments and manage enterprise seats.
- To send transactional email and, with consent, product updates.
- To secure the platform, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Bases (GDPR)
We rely on: **contract** (to provide the service you signed up for), **legitimate interests** (security, service improvement), **consent** (marketing, optional WhatsApp updates), and **legal obligation** (tax, accounting).

## 4. Your Rights
Under UAE PDPL, GDPR, and equivalent frameworks you may:
- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — update your profile in the dashboard or via **{{ PRIVACY_EMAIL }}**.
- **Deletion** — request account deletion. Certificates remain publicly verifiable unless you also request revocation.
- **Portability** — request a machine-readable export of your enrolments, quiz attempts, and certificates.
- **Objection** — object to a specific processing purpose (e.g. marketing) with immediate effect.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing relies on consent.

**Response SLA:** 30 days from receipt (extendable to 60 days for complex requests, with notice). To exercise any right, contact **{{ PRIVACY_EMAIL }}**.

## 5. Data Retention
| Data class | Default retention |

## 6. Sharing & Sub-Processors
We share data only with vetted sub-processors needed to run the service: cloud compute & storage, CDN, transactional email, payments (Stripe), and LLM inference providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) under their zero-training data-processing terms. The current sub-processor list is published on **/trust** and updated with 30 days' notice for enterprise customers.

## 7. International Transfers
